## Your description: the first thing a new reader sees

![Settings, profile form](../../../../assets/playbooks/11-instellingen-profiel-formulier.jpg)

Go to **Settings › Profile**. At the bottom you'll find the **Description** field. This is a very important text, because it's the first thing new visitors see, at the top of your homepage, with the sign-up form right below it. This is where you introduce yourself and convince visitors to subscribe.

### What works well

- **Say why you're an authority**, or what makes you special as a writer. So many people write online that it's important to explain what makes your writing worth it. A bit awkward, I know, but it really helps. Use social proof if you can.
- **Explain what the reader can expect** and what's in it for them. Pick a genre and make it clear: is your newsletter a public mood board, a collection of good links, an open diary, the show notes of your podcast?
- **Say explicitly that they should subscribe.** People look at everything with half their attention, because so much is asking for it. So always spell out every action you want readers to take.

> [!example] How I do it
> In my description I write that I co-founded De Correspondent and that more than 32,000 people already read the newsletter.

> [!note] Note
> This is a different text from the opening text of your emails (that one lives under [Mail](../mail/)) and different again from a page you create yourself.

## The other fields

The same screen has a few more fields.

### Name

Choose an appealing name for your newsletter. **Preferably not just your own name** (unless you're famous), because then only people who already know you will subscribe. Treat your newsletter as a brand. Think of:

- *In Therapie* by Lena Bril
- *I Like to Watch* by TV critic Anke Meijer
- *Docupdate* by documentary critic Helmut Boeijen

### Language

Dutch or English. This is the language of your Lettership and your newsletters. If you write in English, set it to English.

### Time zone

The clock of your publication. It determines the dates readers see on your emails and the times at which you schedule.

### Forward replies to

Readers reply to your newsletter with the reply button in their email client, and only you see those replies. I find all the tips and kind messages an enrichment of my life.

**For most writers this field can simply stay empty:** replies then arrive at the address you use to log in to Lettership. If you'd rather receive them in another inbox, enter that address here.
