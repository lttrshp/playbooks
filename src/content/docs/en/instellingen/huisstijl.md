> [!tip] In short
> - A logo is a nice bonus, not a requirement. **Don't postpone your first newsletters until your logo is done.**
> - Use **transparent PNGs**.
> - Your **social links** appear at the bottom of every email and on your site.

![Settings, branding, logos](../../../../assets/playbooks/12-instellingen-huisstijl-logos.jpg)

Go to **Settings › Branding**. A logo is a nice bonus. It makes your newsletter stand out more and gives your Lettership a face of its own. A nicely designed Lettership immediately signals that the sender takes it seriously. Look at *Kapitale Kanttekeningen* by Milou Brand, for example, with its own logo and a clear visual line.

## Logos

There are four fields.

> [!caution] Use transparent PNGs
> Email clients can't display SVG.

- **Main logo (wordmark):** a wide, horizontal logo for the top of your emails and your site. At least 1280 pixels wide.
- **Footer logo (monogram):** a square mark for the colophon at the bottom of your emails and on your site.
- **The two variants for light backgrounds** are only needed if you've chosen a dark background for your site. Then you probably have a light logo on it, and that won't work in your emails, because those always have a light background. In that case, upload a version of your logo that works well on light.

## Social links

![Settings, branding, social links](../../../../assets/playbooks/13-instellingen-huisstijl-social.jpg)

Below that: your social links (X, Instagram, LinkedIn). They appear at the very bottom of every email and on your Lettership, so people can follow you elsewhere too. They don't distract from the subscribe button.

> [!tip] Subscribers over followers
> The most important thing remains that people subscribe, because unlike a follower on X, Instagram or LinkedIn, you own that email address and can take it to any other platform.
